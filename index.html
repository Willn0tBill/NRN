New Republic Network Awards

How NRNA works

NRNA runs in three-month cycles, three times a year, each split into nominations, the running period, and voting. The final quarter switches to a single Yearly vote instead.

Month 1

Nominations

The community puts forward players, mods, and games for consideration.

Month 2

Running

Nominees are locked in and campaign for support ahead of voting.

Month 3

Voting

The community votes and winners are announced for that cycle.

Q4 is different: Yearly voting

October, November, and December don't run another nominate-run-vote cycle. Instead, that quarter is set aside for NRNA Yearly voting, covering the best of the whole year across all three categories.

Award categories

The same three categories apply to every quarterly cycle and to the Yearly vote.
